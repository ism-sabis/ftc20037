## Team Overview

Standard Deviation is an FTC team from the International School of Minnesota in Eden Prairie. We are competing in BIOBUZZ presented by RTX, part of the FIRST CANOPY season. The 2026-2027 roster has six students, with one additional member profile coming soon.

- Team members: Roshan, Ryan, Asif, Ean, Alex, and Yevhen
- Team composition: 2 seniors and 4 juniors
- Roshan and Ryan have been part of every season shown in this archive
- Asif, Ean, and Alex have been with the team since at least the 2025-2026 season
- Yevhen is joining Standard Deviation this season
- Mentor support: 1 lead mentor with industry engineering experience

## Mission

Our mission is to improve ourselves and our community through outreach, STEM learning, and inspiring younger students to explore robotics and engineering.
