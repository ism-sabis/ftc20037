## Robot Overview {#robot}

Our robot is still in development and does not have a name yet. A gravity-assisted four-bar intake collects game elements, with three rows of surgical tubing guiding them up through the robot. The launcher is designed to LAUNCH both POLLEN and NECTAR into the upward-facing CELL of the HIVE. A pair of weighted flywheels is intended to stay spinning between shots, with the motors maintaining a target RPM and a rear flipper holding each element until release.

The turret-mounted Limelight helps aim the launcher, and an adjustable hood controls the shot trajectory. For FLOWER scoring, an extendable slide guide redirects a shot through the top of a FLOWER, while a separate servo-actuated arm is designed to push POLLEN out through the bottom. The chassis uses mecanum wheels and pocketed 7075 aluminum panels; the team plans to use an odometry pod and IMU with Pedro Pathing for autonomous movement. These are design goals from the current CAD and are still being refined.
